3. Legal bases (UK GDPR)

  • Account and service data: performance of our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Health and wellbeing data: your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)), given separately at sign-up. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.

4. Children's privacy (COPPA notice to parents)

Only adults aged 18 or over may create an account. Children never get their own account. A child may use the "student view" on a parent's signed-in device, where they can see their own profile and record mood check-ins and WHO-5 answers.

  • We obtain your consent as the parent at sign-up, before any information about or from your child is collected.
  • We use children's information only to show it back to you and to your child. We do not disclose it to third parties except the service providers listed in section 6, who process it on our instructions.
  • You can review, export, correct or delete your child's information at any time in the app, and you can stop further collection by deleting the child's profile or your account.
  • We do not require a child to give more information than the activity needs.
  • We keep a written information security programme and a data retention policy for children's information (section 7 and section 8).

Questions about children's information: [privacy email], [postal address], [phone].

5. How assessments work

The "assessment" is calculated by fixed rules from published standards, and every statement names the data it is based on. It is for your information only. It is not medical, psychological or educational advice, and it makes no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

6. Where data is stored and who processes it

  • Database: Google Cloud Firestore, region europe-west2 (London). Google LLC / Google Ireland acts as our processor.
  • Sign-in: Firebase Authentication (Google). Some authentication processing may take place in the United States, covered by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum / EU Standard Contractual Clauses and Google's participation in the Data Privacy Framework.
  • We do not sell or share personal information for advertising, and we do not disclose it to anyone else unless the law requires it.

7. Retention

We keep your data until you delete the record, the child's profile or your account. Deletion in the app removes the data from our database immediately. Provider backups roll over within [30] days.

8. Security

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (Google Cloud).
  • Database rules that let each verified account read and write only its own records.
  • Account deletion requires your password again.
  • If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law, including the ICO under UK GDPR and the FTC under the Health Breach Notification Rule where it applies.

9. Your rights

You can access and export your data (Account → Export all data), correct it (Edit), delete it (Delete, or Account → Delete account), and withdraw consent (Account → Delete account). Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restriction, objection and portability, and US state privacy rights. Contact us at [privacy email]. We reply within one month. In the UK you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

10. Changes

We will post updates here. If a change affects how we use health, wellbeing or children's data, we will ask for your consent again the next time you sign in.
